04 · The Acapuro experience

Start playing. Keep buying simple.

One reusable builder section, available on the Home and on its own dedicated page.

  1. 01

    Piece

    Necklace, bracelet, anklet or keychain. Show size choices only where needed: necklace 16, 18 or 20 in.

  2. 02

    Chain

    Compare around ten chains with real photos and prices for the selected size.

  3. 03

    Charms

    Add 1–7. Suggest 1, 3, 5 or 7 without limiting choice; review the composition.

Tap first. Drag second.

Tap to add automatically; move or remove without dragging. Keep count, price and review easy to reach.

No hidden choices.

Show the base chain/size price, included allowance, extra charm prices and live total. No unapproved paid defaults.

04 / Focus

A clear search promise.

Keep the interactive builder first on Home. Give the dedicated Builder page a descriptive H1, title and crawlable copy; give each real collection its own descriptive URL and title.

05 / Relevance

Avoid chasing broad traffic.

Personalized jewelry shows 5,000 but spans products Acapuro does not sell. Competitor brands, licensed characters and DIY supply queries are not launch targets.

Evidence, limitations & SEO launch checklist

All six exports contain 241 rows and 149 unique terms. The rounded averages are 5,000, 500, 50 or 0; six unique terms have missing averages. Monthly breakdown columns are empty. The files do not confirm the geographic filter. No volume is claimed for terms without data, including keychain brand activation and several city variations.

Paid competition index: custom charm necklace 100, custom charm bracelet 99, mobile charm bar 0, charm bar for events 18 and charm bar Miami 22. These are advertising signals, not SEO difficulty. Similar phrases may share underlying search data; do not add their averages.

Before launch: map Squarespace URLs to Shopify redirects, set unique titles and descriptions, use descriptive product images and alt text, confirm canonical URLs and indexable content, exclude duplicate builder share states from indexing, submit the sitemap and verify Search Console. Recheck search intent and geographic targeting before final keyword selection.

Measurement & scope notes

Use builder_started, piece_type_selected, chain_selected, charm_added, preset_loaded, design_shared and builder_add_to_cart alongside standard Shopify commerce events. Track event_request_submitted as a lead, not a confirmed booking. Never send personal form data to analytics.
